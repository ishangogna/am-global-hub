# AM Global Hub — What's New

Welcome to the new and improved AM Global Hub! Here's everything that's been added to make your corporate gifting experience smoother, faster, and more personal.

---

## Create an Account in Seconds

No passwords to remember. Just enter your email, get a one-time code, and you're in. We'll ask for your name and phone number so we can personalise your experience.

---

## Save Products You Love

See something interesting? Tap the heart icon on any product card to save it to your wishlist. Come back anytime to find your saved items on your Account page — perfect for comparing options before placing an order.

---

## Request a Quote Instantly

Hit "Request a Quote" on any product and we'll open a form with your details already filled in (if you're logged in). Fill in the quantity, tap Submit, and it goes straight to our WhatsApp — no waiting, no emails lost in spam.

---

## Track Your Quote Requests

Every quote you submit is saved to your Account page. You can see the product, quantity, date, and current status:
- **Pending** — we've received it
- **Responded** — we've sent you pricing
- **Confirmed** — order confirmed
- **Completed** — delivered!

---

## Recently Viewed Products

Forgot the name of something you looked at earlier? The Products page now shows your last 5 viewed items at the bottom so you can pick up right where you left off.

---

## Search as You Type

Start typing in the search bar on the Products page and you'll see instant suggestions with product images and prices. Click any suggestion to jump straight to that product.

---

## Share Products with Your Team

Every product page now has a Share button. Copy the link or send it directly via WhatsApp — great for sharing options with colleagues before making a decision.

---

## Discounts & Deals

Look out for green "% OFF" badges on product cards. When a product is on offer, you'll see both the original price (strikethrough) and the discounted price clearly displayed.

---

## WhatsApp — Always One Tap Away

You'll notice a green WhatsApp button in the bottom-right corner of every page. Tap it anytime to start a conversation with us instantly.

---

## Newsletter

Opted in to our newsletter? We'll send you our latest product catalogue straight to your inbox. You can subscribe or unsubscribe anytime from your Account page.

---

## Improved Contact Page

Our contact page now has everything in one place:
- Send us a message (via WhatsApp)
- Our office address with Google Maps
- Phone number (tap to call)
- Instagram (@amglobalhub)
- WhatsApp quick-connect
- Business hours (with today highlighted)

---

## Faster, Smoother Browsing

- Pages load faster with a gold progress bar at the top
- Product images load as you scroll (not all at once)
- Pagination keeps the catalogue organised — 12 products per page

---

## Your Account, Your Way

From your Account page you can:
- Update your name and phone number
- See your saved products
- View your quote history and status
- Toggle newsletter subscription on/off
- Sign out

---

We're constantly improving. If you have feedback or ideas, just WhatsApp us — we're always listening.
